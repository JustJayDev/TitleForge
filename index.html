FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — the app is free and open source. You need a channel to work on, and an AI key held by the Developer Vault (one-time setup, described in the README).

Is my data safe?

Your Google token is held in the page's memory only — never in localStorage, sessionStorage or cookies. It is cleared the moment you disconnect or close the tab. We never store it anywhere.

How many videos can I rename?

As many as you have. The YouTube API gives 10,000 free quota units/day per project.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Connect with Google, review the preview, click apply. That's it.
